ABOUT US

The People Behind The Mission

We're a team of educators, writers, and changemakers working to make African youth future-ready — one classroom, one child at a time.

Our Story

The Luminary Foundation for Education was born out of a simple but urgent observation: too many bright, capable children in Nigeria were being failed not by a lack of potential, but by a lack of access to quality education and the writing and thinking skills needed to thrive beyond the classroom.

What started as a small volunteer effort to tutor and mentor students has grown into a foundation with a clear mission — to equip children with the skills to shine as luminaries and lead as changemakers, across Nigeria and beyond.

Today, we work directly with schools, communities, and families to close the learning poverty gap and prepare African youth for the 21st-century world.

Our Values

These principles guide every program we run and every decision we make.

Excellence

We hold ourselves and our students to a high standard, because every child deserves the best we can give.

Integrity

We do right by our students, families, and partners, even when no one is watching.

Consistency

Transformation takes time — we show up for our students today, tomorrow, and for the long run.

Community

We believe lasting change happens together — with families, schools, and partners walking alongside us.
